OKX and ICE open tokenized securities venue with 113 assets

OKX and ICE opened a tokenized securities venue with 113 assets confirmed for staged listing, while objections from 67 companies narrowed which shares could appear.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 24, 2026 · 11:54 UTC3 min read

OKX and Intercontinental Exchange (ICE) have opened OKXICE, a venue where eligible U.S. customers can trade tokens representing securities through blockchain liquidity pools. The launch comes after 67 companies filed objections to having tokenized versions of their shares offered there, according to The Block’s report on the launch.

The distinction between the headline count and what is available at once matters: OKXICE has confirmed 113 assets for listing, including stocks, exchange-traded funds and other products, but they are due to become available on a rolling basis. CoinDesk reported that the lineup includes 59 Direxion ETFs and that the full asset list had not been provided.

How OKXICE routes a trade

Instead of matching buy and sell orders in a conventional order book, the venue uses automated market maker pools built with Uniswap v4 on X Layer, OKX’s Ethereum Layer 2 network. A customer’s trade executes against assets in a pool, much like drawing from shared inventory. Smart contracts restrict trading and liquidity provision to eligible wallets.

Securitize provides the tokenization infrastructure. Each token represents a security entitlement backed one-to-one by an underlying share, according to The Block. The venue says holders receive applicable shareholder rights and economic benefits, including dividends, voting rights and participation in corporate actions. Customers keep the tokens in self-custodial wallets.

Access is limited to eligible U.S.-based OKX customers. The venue operates under the SEC’s five-year Innovation Exemption, which permits eligible platforms to test limited onchain trading of tokenized U.S.-listed stocks under specified conditions, The Block reported.

What the 113-asset count covers

  • 113 confirmed assets: Stocks, ETFs and other exchange-traded products are scheduled for phased listing, rather than all being available on day one.
  • 59 Direxion ETFs: The fund manager plans to bring these products to the venue, according to CoinDesk’s report on the confirmed assets and participants.
  • 67 company objections: The Block cited OKXICE’s latest public notice. The objections prevent tokenized versions of those companies’ shares from being offered on this venue.

That resistance shaped the initial roster, while new participants expanded the planned listings. CoinDesk reported that Fidelity Investments, eToro, Strive and Vida Global were among the newly confirmed participants; earlier names included Coinbase, Circle, Robinhood and Strategy.

The change is that OKXICE has moved from a planned venue to a live one, with a larger confirmed pipeline than its initial list. The next concrete measure is how quickly those 113 assets become available and which securities are included in each stage.
