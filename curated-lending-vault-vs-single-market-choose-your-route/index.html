Curated lending vault vs single market: choose your route

Curated lending vault vs lending to a single market directly: a vault spreads and rebalances funds for a fee; direct lending keeps one market under your control.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 28, 2026 · 01:08 UTC4 min read

A curated lending vault spreads a deposit across markets selected by a Curator, while direct lending keeps funds in one market you choose, with no curator fee.

With Morpho Blue, you can supply to a lending market yourself or deposit into a vault that allocates funds across enabled markets and other supported yield sources. Morpho vaults use a Curator to set which markets are enabled and their supply caps; an Allocator can then place and move funds within those boundaries.

Who picks the risk, and how broad is the exposure?

In direct lending, you pick one market and keep your deposit exposed to it until you withdraw. Your position does not move to another market on its own. You choose the collateral, oracle and LLTV—the market’s loan-to-value setting—and need to review those details before supplying.

A vault changes who makes those choices. The Curator defines the markets and caps, and the Allocator can move funds among the enabled options. That means the vault can spread funds across markets, but the curator’s selection determines the range. A market you have never reviewed could be included if the Curator enabled it.

Think of direct lending as choosing one shelf for your deposit. A vault is closer to letting someone arrange items across several shelves, within the space you have allowed. The broader placement can diversify exposure across markets, but it does not mean every vault holds the same markets or that you personally chose each one.

That movement is the key trade-off. A direct position stays in its chosen market until you withdraw. A vault’s Allocator can shift funds to any market the Curator has enabled, so the mix can change without you selecting each destination. To use a vault, you are trusting the Curator’s market choices and the Allocator to act within them.

What do you pay, and what work stays yours?

Direct supply to a Morpho Blue market involves no curator or vault fee. You keep a fixed, single-market exposure and do the selection and review yourself. That takes work: you need to assess the collateral, oracle and LLTV for the market you choose, and decide whether that particular exposure still suits you.

A curated vault charges a performance fee. In return, the Curator and Allocator handle market selection and allocation within the vault’s rules. You do not have to choose each market yourself, but you also give up the control of keeping funds in one specific market. The fee is part of the cost of using that managed route; compare it with the effort and control you want to keep.

One practical distinction is how much checking happens before deposit. A direct lender can inspect one market and know that the position remains there until withdrawal. A vault depositor must assess the Curator’s enabled-market choices and supply caps, then accept that the Allocator may move funds among them. The vault reduces the need to make each allocation decision, but it does not remove the need to understand the mandate you are trusting.

Which route fits your situation?

Choose a curated vault if you want market selection and rebalancing handled for you and are comfortable with the Curator’s choices, including the possibility of allocation to a market you did not review yourself. The performance fee is the cost to weigh against that reduced day-to-day selection work.

Choose direct lending if you want one known market exposure, are prepared to review its collateral, oracle and LLTV, and prefer to avoid a curator fee. Your funds remain in that market until you withdraw, so the control is clearer and the review is yours to do.

The choice comes down to effort, control and cost: a vault delegates market selection and can move funds across enabled markets for a performance fee; direct lending keeps one market fixed without a curator fee. If you will not review markets yourself, choose a vault you trust. If you want one known exposure and no fee, lend directly. The constraint is whether you are willing to make and maintain the market choice yourself.
