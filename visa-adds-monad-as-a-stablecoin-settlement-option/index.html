Visa adds Monad as a stablecoin settlement option

Visa has added Monad as a blockchain route for participating issuers and acquirers to settle obligations in stablecoins, with privacy tools planned for later.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 10, 2026 · 22:17 UTC2 min read

Visa has added the Monad blockchain as a route for participating issuers and acquirers to settle obligations in stablecoins, giving them another network for moving funds between financial institutions. The Monad Foundation’s November 10 announcement says the option is available now. The settlement runs behind the cardholder’s familiar Visa experience; it does not add a new checkout option for shoppers.

What changes when a payment is settled on Monad?

After a card payment is processed, the financial institutions involved must settle what they owe through Visa. In this arrangement, participating issuers and acquirers can use stablecoins on Monad to complete that settlement. Instead of relying only on banking rails that traditionally operate on a five-business-day schedule, the institutions can move funds over a blockchain that runs continuously, including weekends and holidays, according to the foundation.

Monad says transactions reach finality in 800 milliseconds, meaning the network treats them as settled and no longer reversible. For comparison, think of the settlement network as a route between institutions: Visa’s partners can now choose Monad as one more route. The foundation says its Ethereum Virtual Machine compatibility may let institutions extend existing Ethereum custody, wallet and compliance tools to Monad, rather than build new integrations from scratch.

Which stablecoins and institutions are included?

The announcement does not name the stablecoins available for settlement on Monad or identify participating issuers and acquirers. Unchained’s report likewise notes that those details were not disclosed. Visa’s broader program had more than 225 stablecoin-linked card programs live globally as of September, according to figures in the foundation’s release; that number is not a count of Monad users or payments.

The foundation also says Monad’s “Private Settlement” feature is planned for the coming months, not live yet. It is designed to let institutions keep commercially sensitive settlement details private while allowing them to share those details with auditors and regulators. The feature is intended to run on the same network as other transactions, keeping private activity connected to the network’s liquidity.

What should Visa partners watch next?

The immediate change is an additional blockchain option for institutions that settle with Visa in stablecoins. The next details to watch are which stablecoins and partners use the Monad route, and when the planned privacy feature becomes available. The announcement does not say when particular institutions will begin settling on Monad.
