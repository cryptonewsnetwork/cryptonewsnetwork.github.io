Injective’s Veyra upgrade enters vote before Nov. 24 target

Injective opened a governance vote on Veyra, a proposed v1.21.0 upgrade that would halt the chain at block 193,145,000 on Nov. 24 if approved.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 13, 2026 · 11:58 UTC2 min read

Injective has opened a governance vote on Veyra, a proposed software upgrade that would stop the chain at block 193,145,000 and restart it on version 1.21.0 if approved. The Veyra proposal says the halt is targeted for November 24; the vote opened November 12 and is scheduled to close November 16.

The sequence is straightforward: eligible voting power considers the software proposal, and the network changes only if the proposal passes. Injective’s upgrade tracker describes the next steps for major upgrades: the chain halts at the specified block, validators switch to the new application binary, and consensus resumes. The tracker lists Veyra as proposal 717 and version 1.21.0.

The proposal describes several intended changes. It would expand Injective’s MultiVM architecture with Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC) capabilities, allowing assets to move between Injective’s Ethereum Virtual Machine environment and other connected networks. The proposal says this could support cross-chain liquidity and institutional issuance of tokenized assets.

It also proposes request-for-quote infrastructure and expanded atomic-swap functionality. A request for quote lets a trader ask for a price before completing a trade; the proposal presents these tools as groundwork for more complex institutional trading and settlement. These are proposed capabilities in the upgrade, not a claim that every related service is already live.

Veyra is also described as preparation for CypherOS, Injective’s planned native privacy platform. The proposal says the upgrade builds on earlier work and supports a roadmap for confidential trading and financial transactions. It does not say CypherOS itself launches with Veyra.

At the proposal page’s displayed voting snapshot, participation was 21.28%, below the stated 33.4% quorum. Of the 11,939,770 INJ recorded as voted, 55.5% was marked yes and 44.5% abstain; no votes or vetoes were recorded. The page still listed the proposal as voting, so those figures describe a live tally, not a final result.

The immediate change is that Veyra has entered governance review, while its proposed activation remains conditional on the vote. The next markers are the November 16 voting close and, if approved, the targeted halt at block 193,145,000 on November 24.
