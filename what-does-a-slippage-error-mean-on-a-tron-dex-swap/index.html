What does a slippage error mean on a TRON DEX swap?

A slippage tolerance exceeded error on a TRON DEX means the swap's output fell below your minimum; refresh the quote and adjust tolerance only as needed.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 19, 2026 · 12:28 UTC4 min read

A slippage tolerance exceeded error on a TRON DEX means the swap would return less than your minimum received by execution time, so the contract reverted it and your input tokens stayed in your wallet. SunSwap is a concrete example: its TRC-20 swaps use liquidity pools, where the pool quote and minimum-output check determine whether a trade can complete. Start with a fresh quote, raise tolerance only enough to cover the cause, then confirm the retry succeeded and the output arrived; a revert still spends the Energy or TRX used up to that point.

How the minimum-output check causes a revert

A DEX router calculates the minimum output from the quoted amount and your slippage tolerance. The formula is quoted output multiplied by one minus the tolerance. With a quote of 100 tokens and a 1% tolerance, the minimum received is 99 tokens. If execution would deliver less than 99, the router reverts the swap.

The quote is an estimate based on the pool when you request it. Other trades can change the pool price before your transaction executes. A large order can also move that price as it trades through the pool. The resulting output may fall below your minimum even though the quote looked acceptable moments earlier.

A transfer-tax token creates another shortfall. The token takes a percentage when it is transferred, so the amount credited to you is lower than the pool’s quoted output. The tax applies on every attempt. If your tolerance is below the tax’s effect on the output, retrying with the same setting can fail again.

After a revert, the swap’s token transfer is rolled back: your input tokens remain in your wallet. The network work already spent is different. A reverted TRON transaction still consumes Energy or TRX used up to the revert, so the transaction can cost you network resources without completing the trade. TronLink shows the wallet-side transaction; TRONSCAN can help you inspect its transaction status.

A common mistake is to see a failed swap and assume the input tokens were sold. Say you tried to swap 100 TRC-20 tokens, and the trade reverted because the output missed your minimum. You might expect a smaller balance, but the token transfer was rolled back. The network cost was spent; the swap amount stayed in your wallet.

Related questions

What slippage should I set on SunSwap?

Use a tolerance that covers the price movement or token tax you expect, while keeping the minimum output meaningful. There is no single setting that fits every token and trade. Refresh the quote first; if a tax is the cause, account for that tax rather than repeatedly retrying below it. Very high tolerance can allow a worse fill, so raise it only as far as the cause requires.

Why did my swap fail but still charge TRX?

The contract reverted the swap when its minimum-output condition was not met, but the TRON transaction had already used network resources. The input tokens stayed in your wallet; the Energy or TRX spent up to the revert was not returned.

For a retry, refresh the quote, identify whether price movement or a transfer tax caused the shortfall, and adjust tolerance only enough to address it. That avoids repeating a tax-related failure or accepting a needlessly poor minimum output.

More answers about failed swaps

Why does SunSwap say insufficient output amount?

“Insufficient output amount” means the amount calculated at execution fell below the minimum output set for the swap. A changed pool price or a token transfer tax can cause that shortfall.

How do I find a token's buy and sell tax?

Check the token’s official information and contract details, or compare the amount sent with the amount received in separate buy and sell transactions. TRONSCAN transaction data can help with that comparison. Buy and sell taxes may differ, so check each direction.

Can a failed TRON transaction be refunded?

The reverted swap does not transfer your input tokens, but Energy or TRX already spent up to the revert is still consumed. That network cost is not refunded.
