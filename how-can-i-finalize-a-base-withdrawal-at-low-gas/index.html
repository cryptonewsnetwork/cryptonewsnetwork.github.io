How can I finalize a Base withdrawal at low gas?

To finalize a Base withdrawal when Ethereum gas is low, time prove and finalize for a low base fee, then cap each transaction’s max fee in your wallet.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 27, 2026 · 23:14 UTC4 min read

A Base withdrawal started on Base reaches Ethereum at lower cost when you send the prove and finalize transactions while Ethereum’s base fee is low and cap each transaction’s max fee near that target. The withdrawal has three steps: initiate on Base, prove on Ethereum, then finalize on Ethereum. Keep ETH in the withdrawing wallet on Ethereum, because both L1 transactions use it to pay gas.

How do I time the prove and finalize transactions?

Send each Ethereum transaction when the base fee is near its daily low, and set a max fee that lets it wait for that price. Under EIP-1559, the fee paid in ETH equals gas used multiplied by the effective gas price, which combines the base fee and priority fee. Ethereum demand moves the base fee; for the same prove or finalize action, the gas units are fixed, so timing changes the cost through the gas price.

  1. Start with the Base withdrawal already initiated. Keep its transaction hash handy and confirm the withdrawing wallet has ETH on Ethereum. The initiation is on Base; the two fee-sensitive steps happen on Ethereum.
  2. Open the bridge transaction list. Find the withdrawal using its hash and follow its status. A prove transaction becomes available once a state proposal covering that withdrawal has been posted to Ethereum. Until then, the withdrawal is waiting for that proposal.
  3. Wait for a low base fee, then send prove. Read the current base fee on Etherscan Gas Tracker and look for a point near the day’s low. In your wallet’s advanced fee settings, choose a low priority fee and set a max fee just above your target base fee, leaving room for the priority fee. Submit prove when the wallet shows a fee cap you are willing to pay.
  4. Let a low-capped transaction wait when needed. A max fee is a ceiling, not the amount automatically charged. If your cap is below the current base fee, the transaction can remain pending instead of failing. For instance, say the base fee rises after you submit prove with a low cap: the transaction waits in the wallet. When the base fee falls to a level the cap can cover, it can confirm, and the effective gas price determines the fee you pay. A cap set too low may wait longer, so choose a ceiling you can accept and be prepared to adjust it if your timing changes.
  5. Wait out the challenge period, then send finalize. Base mainnet has a seven-day challenge period. After it has elapsed, return to the same withdrawal in the transaction list, check Etherscan Gas Tracker again, and submit finalize with the same approach: a low priority fee and a max fee just above the base fee you are targeting. Finalize is a separate Ethereum transaction, so it gets its own fee and its own opportunity to wait for lower gas.
  6. Confirm the result. The withdrawal is complete when its status shows completed and the ETH balance on Ethereum rises. Open the prove and finalize transactions on Etherscan to see the fees paid for each.

This timing method applies to the Ethereum side of a Base bridge withdrawal: it affects when prove and finalize are sent and the fee settings on those transactions. The linked Base bridge topic covers moving supported assets between Ethereum and Base, the broader context for the withdrawal steps above.

Why is the prove button not available yet?

The prove button becomes available after an Ethereum state proposal covers the withdrawal. Use the bridge transaction list to track the withdrawal while that proposal is pending; the seven-day challenge period follows the required onchain steps and must elapse before finalization.

Can Across skip the 7-day wait?

Across is a separate route to consider when comparing ways to move assets, but this withdrawal’s Base mainnet challenge period is seven days. This procedure explains how to reduce the Ethereum gas paid for prove and finalize; it does not change that period.

  • Withdrawal transaction hash: ready when you can find the initiated withdrawal in the bridge transaction list.
  • ETH on Ethereum in the withdrawing wallet: ready when the wallet shows enough ETH to pay for prove and finalize.
