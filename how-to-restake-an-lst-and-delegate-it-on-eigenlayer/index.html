How to restake an LST and delegate it on EigenLayer

First time restaking on EigenLayer: fund a wallet, deposit a supported LST, delegate it to an operator, and verify the balance on Ethereum.

The Crypto Front Page DeskNov 18, 2026 · 17:20 UTC4 min read

To restake on eigen layer, fund a self-custody wallet with ETH for gas and a supported liquid staking token, deposit the token, then delegate the position to an operator. Use eigen layer at the deposit stage to put the LST into its matching restaking strategy. Deposit and delegation are separate transactions, so the position is incomplete until both confirm.

Prepare the wallet and token

A first restake needs a self-custody wallet, ETH for transaction fees, and an LST the protocol currently supports. Keep enough ETH for several transactions: approving the token, depositing it, and delegating each require a wallet confirmation. The supported LST is the asset you will deposit; stETH and rETH are examples, but check current support before acquiring one.

Start with an empty wallet by sending ETH to its address, then acquire the supported LST. Open the EigenLayer app with the wallet set to Ethereum mainnet. Confirm that the wallet holds both assets before starting. Native restaking is a separate route: it requires a 32 ETH validator whose withdrawal credentials point to an EigenPod.

Approve and deposit the LST

The LST route moves through approval first and deposit second. Approval lets the StrategyManager move the chosen token amount; the deposit places that amount into the corresponding strategy and credits restaking shares to your wallet.

  1. Approve the token. Select the supported LST and approve the amount you intend to deposit. This is the first transaction. Wait for its confirmation before moving on.
  2. Deposit into the strategy. Submit the deposit transaction for that LST. This is a separate transaction from approval. Wait for confirmation; the restaked balance should then appear in the account.

Think of approval as authorizing a transfer and deposit as making it: signing the first does not complete the second. The StrategyManager accounts for deposits by strategy, so the token and its matching strategy must correspond.

Choose an operator and delegate

Delegation assigns all of the wallet’s restaked shares to one operator. Review which AVSs an operator runs and the commission it sets for each AVS, then choose an operator whose services and terms you understand. Delegated stake can face slashing risk if an operator fails to meet a service’s requirements.

Submit the delegation transaction after the deposit confirms. Delegation is not included in the deposit transaction, and one wallet cannot split its shares between two operators. To spread restaked assets across operators, use separate wallets and delegate each wallet’s position to one operator. The Delegation contract records that assignment; a deposit made before delegation remains undelegated until this step completes.

Say a new user funds a wallet, acquires stETH, and deposits it. After the approval confirms, the deposit confirms and the restaked balance appears. The user compares operators by AVSs and per-AVS commission, picks one, and signs the delegation transaction. Once it confirms, that wallet’s restaked shares are assigned to the chosen operator.

Verify the completed position

A completed first restake shows both the deposited amount and chosen operator in the dashboard. Confirm those details there, then open the transaction in a block explorer and make sure its status is successful. For a clear record, verify the approval, deposit, and delegation confirmations separately.

  • No ETH for gas: The wallet may hold the LST but lack ETH to submit a transaction. Add ETH before continuing.
  • Wrong network: Switch the wallet to Ethereum mainnet, then reconnect and retry the intended action.
  • Approval only: An approved token allowance is not a restaked deposit. Submit the deposit and wait for its confirmation.
  • Deposit without delegation: The balance is deposited, but it is not assigned to an operator. Complete the separate delegation transaction.

The finish line is a confirmed restaked balance displayed as delegated to the operator you chose. That entry tells you the deposit and delegation both took effect; keep the operator’s AVSs and commissions in view as you monitor the position.
