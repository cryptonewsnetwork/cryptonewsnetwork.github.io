About Crypto Front Page

Crypto Front Page reports news across the crypto industry: Bitcoin and Ethereum, layer-2 networks, stablecoins, exchanges, DeFi protocols, security incidents and the rules governments write for all of them. Each story starts with how the system in question works, then sets out what changed and who it affects.

We are independent and not affiliated with any project, exchange or token issuer we cover. We work from primary sources such as filings, code repositories, on-chain records and official statements, and we correct errors openly, noting the change on the story. Nothing we publish is financial advice.
